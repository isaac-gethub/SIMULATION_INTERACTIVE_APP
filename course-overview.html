Understand the Method Before You Begin

TIB Academy is deliberately different from a conventional online course. You are not moving through lectures and quizzes. You are moving through the lifecycle of a controls project and gradually taking more responsibility for the work.

FOUNDATION → BUILD IT → LEAD IT → OWN IT

Four Learning Layers

FOUNDATION

B0-B10 controls theory: goals, risks, control types and nature, engagement types, S/4HANA lifecycle, functional-design reading, testing decisions, AI and practitioner capstone.

BUILD IT

9 practical activities: Environment, Information Flows, Business Processes, Risks, Controls, Owners, Evidence, Testing and Results.

LEAD IT

8 leadership activities: Take Command, Manage Team, Review Work, Technical Challenge, Manage Audit, Remediation, Executive Governance and 30-Day Capstone.

OWN IT

Independent employment-readiness gate using an inherited environment, conflicting priorities, weak evidence, audit pressure and verbal defense.

The TIB Learning Engine

SEE → LISTEN → OBSERVE → BUILD → COMPARE → CONNECT
SEE

See the finished work product and its purpose.

LISTEN

Hear the work explained as a first-person project story.

OBSERVE

Inspect realistic evidence, examples and source documents.

BUILD

Perform the work in actual project workbooks.

COMPARE

Calibrate against model reasoning and instructor feedback.

CONNECT

Trace how one artifact creates the next project decision.

One Continuous Project Environment

Corvane Energy Group provides a consistent fictitious company, people structure, SAP landscape, interfaces, processes, risks, controls, evidence, meetings, audit issues and management pressure. The learner is not solving disconnected examples.

  • Map systems, processes, risks, controls, owners and evidence.

  • Take command of an inherited controls environment and defend your position.

Alignment to Current Job Statements of Work

Current 2026 SAP controls/security governance postings repeatedly call for combinations of SOX/ITGC, audit readiness, risk assessment, SoD/access governance, S/4HANA/Fiori/cloud awareness, remediation, automation/continuous monitoring, dashboards and stakeholder/team leadership. TIB Academy is deliberately built around those management and controls-delivery responsibilities.

How Readiness Is Tested

The final gate does not provide a clean answer sequence. The candidate must independently ASSESS → PRIORITIZE → SCOPE → DELEGATE → CHALLENGE → REVIEW → DECIDE → ESCALATE → REPORT → DEFEND. Final outcomes are READY, READY WITH COACHING or NOT YET READY.
